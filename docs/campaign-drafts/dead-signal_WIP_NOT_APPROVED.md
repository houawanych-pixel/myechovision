# Dead Signal — campaign skeleton (WIP, NOT APPROVED)

Bones only. Checkpoints that must happen, A to B; everything between them is played live.
Structure borrowed (not copied) from three proven shapes: the rescue-mission-gone-wrong (Aliens), the
amnesia-in-a-locked-down-facility mystery (Resident Evil), and lonely room-by-room exploration of a hostile
station (Metroid). All names, places and details are our own.

**Logline:** A bounty-hunter crew answers an SOS and finds you alone on a drifting ship with no memory. They
take you with them to a dead station. As your memories come back in pieces, you realize the station is the
place you escaped — and someone there is growing something as a weapon.

## The crew (who you meet)
- **Kara Venn** — crew chief, blunt, protective, keeps her people alive first.
- **Tobin "Rook" Ashe** — tech support, jittery, talks when scared, best with locks and systems.
- **Sera Vale** — veteran hunter, dead calm, nothing scares her (or so it looks).
- **Unit Seven** — the crew's android medic. Nobody trusts it after "last time". It earns its place.
- **Mira** — a child survivor found at the station (the one you go back for).
- Hidden: **Director Hale** — the company man aboard the station (or on comms) running the weapon program.

## Checkpoints
Memory fragments are marked **[M1]…[M5]**. Each returns in pieces, as a flash the narrator reads.

0. **Intro — Found.** Your little ship drifts, beacon crying SOS. You wake in a cold pod as a crew cuts
   through the hull. You don't know your name. **[M1]** A single image: a white corridor, red light, running.
1. **Taken in.** The bounty hunters bring you aboard and give you a place. Kara decides you're crew until
   proven otherwise. Unit Seven checks you over; everyone watches it, not you.
2. **The second signal.** A station nearby is answering on the same old frequency. The job pays.
   They head there. You feel sick and don't know why. **[M2]** The station's name on a door sign — you've
   read it before.
3. **Dead station.** Docking. No lights, no people, doors sealed. Sounds start: slow booms deep inside,
   something skittering through the walls. The crew hears it too ("Did you hear that?").
4. **Locked out.** Every route to the bridge is sealed. Unit Seven crawls the vents alone to open them from
   the other side — where the skittering lives. It makes it. First respect earned.
5. **Power on.** On the bridge, Rook restores power to read the logs. The lights come up… and every door
   unlocks, both ways. The station wakes. Alarms, a looping company message, the thing starts hunting.
   **[M3]** You know this alarm. You know the way to the lower labs without a map.
6. **The labs.** Logs show the species being bred and studied. You find Mira hiding in the ducts.
   **[M4]** You worked here. You were the one who tried to get the test subjects out, and they caught you.
7. **The twist.** **[M5]** It all comes back: you weren't drifting by accident. You escaped *this* station,
   and the company behind it, and you have been carried straight back to the one place you ran from.
   Director Hale comes on comms and knows your name. The station's "job" the crew answered was bait from
   the company to bring back anything alive. The crew had no idea; they're on your side, and now they're
   targets too.
8. **Losses and the last stand.** The hunt closes in; the crew is cut down to the few who matter. Unit Seven
   holds a door and is torn in half — and still talks you through the escape route.
9. **Go back.** At the shuttle, safe, you realize Mira was taken below. Everyone says leave. You go back.
10. **Ending.** You bring Mira out, with what is left of Unit Seven and whoever is still standing, and you
    choose what happens to the station and the proof of what Hale built.

## Notes
- Twist: the crew that finds you is **not** the threat and never was. The **destination** — the station and
  the company behind it — is what you fled. The horror is where you're going, not who saved you.
- Group reactions: one surprised, one scared, one calm (Sera) — same sound, different people.
- Sound: booms and wall-skittering start at checkpoint 3 and build; music turns scary, then epic chase at 5.
- To play it now: Create → Your story → paste checkpoint 0 as the Beginning, 1–9 as chapters, 10 as the
  ending. The narrator reads each one as you reach it.
