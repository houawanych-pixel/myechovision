# The Kumite (world draft, not approved)

*Name: **Kumite** (組手, Japanese for "sparring", literally "grappling hands"). It's the secret, invitation-only fight tournament in **Bloodsport** (1988, Jean-Claude Van Damme). Kickboxer is a different Van Damme film, with Tong Po and Muay Thai, and has no Kumite. In Bloodsport it is unarmed, but ours is a sword tournament, which makes it its own thing.*

## The idea
A hidden tournament where **humans** and **dragon half-breeds** fight with blades. You arrive as an outsider, meet the fighters (each with a persona, voice and clips), and fight your way up the ladder. It's built on the arena-combat plan (`arena-combat_WIP_NOT_APPROVED.md`): an open skybox, a 3D floor, an opponent who always faces you, dodge to make them miss, move in to land a hit, life meters.

## Every fighter's clip kit
Same rules for all: **use their master picture as the exact first frame and end on the same pose**, locked camera, same size, **plain green background** (blue if they wear green), **full body**, **vertical 2:3**, **480 × 720 if animated, 720 × 1080 if realistic** (these are realistic, so 720).

1. **Talk + idle**, 15 s: use **Copy prompt** in their character editor. The app makes the voice, talking loop and idle loop.
2. **Attack 1**, 3–4 s
3. **Attack 2 / special**, 3–4 s
4. **Hurt**, 2–3 s
5. **Knockout**, 3–4 s
6. **Victory**, 3–4 s

Prompt frame for clips 2–6 (paste their move in the brackets):
```
Use the provided image as the exact first frame. Same character, outfit, framing and plain green background the whole time. Locked-off camera, no zoom, full body in frame, the character stays the same size.
[MOVE]
Then they return to the identical starting pose by the end, so the clip loops.
Length: [3 to 4] seconds. Vertical portrait 2:3, 720 x 1080. Sound: only the action's own sound and a short grunt, no music, no voice.
```

### Yuki: moves
- Attack 1: draws her katana from the sash and makes one lightning-fast diagonal cut toward the camera, then slides it back into the scabbard.
- Special: a spinning horizontal cut with a pale arc of wind trailing the blade.
- Hurt: struck, her hat tips, she staggers back a step and steadies.
- Knockout: struck hard, she drops to one knee, then falls to her side.
- Victory: sheathes the sword slowly and bows her head, the hat hiding her eyes.

### Kasumi: moves
*Fight outfit: white halter top, white miniskirt, white heels, black-scabbard katana (her square 1:1 fight picture). Her talking picture wears white trousers; that's fine, the fight clips all start from the fight picture.*
- Attack 1: a quick one-handed slash, white sleeves trailing.
- Attack 2: a leaping cut, landing in a crouch.
- **Special: Mirage**. Fog rolls in, she multiplies into many copies, and they all strike. Her two clips show this. **Use clip 1** (sword drawn, fog, clones spreading): it reads as an attack. Clip 2 is lighter and dance-like.
- Hurt, Knockout, Victory as the template.

**Specials are cinematics.** A special move doesn't need green screen or a loop. When she lands it, the fight cuts to her full-screen special clip (widescreen, real background, like these two), then back to the duel and the damage lands. So her two clips can be used as they are; other fighters' specials can be made the same way (cinematic, about 5–10 s, widescreen).

### The armoured samurai: moves
- Attack 1: raises the long katana overhead with both hands and brings it down in a heavy cut toward the camera.
- Special: a wide sweeping horizontal cut, armour plates clanking.
- Hurt: struck in the chest plate, rocks back, sparks fly off the armour.
- Knockout: falls to his knees, sword planted in the ground, then topples.
- Victory: rests the blade on his shoulder and stares down the camera.

### The Ryu-style fighter: moves (no sword)
- Attack 1: a fast straight punch toward the camera.
- Attack 2: a high roundhouse kick.
- Uppercut: crouches and launches a rising uppercut, leaving the ground.
- Fireball: draws both hands back to the hip, then thrusts them forward and releases a glowing ball of blue energy toward the camera.
- Hurt, Knockout, Victory as above (victory: a raised fist, headband tails fluttering).

*Keep this one original: his own name and look (not Ryu's white gi and red headband), so he's yours.*

### Dragon half-breed: template
- Look: human build with dragon traits (scaled skin, horns, slit eyes, maybe folded wings, clawed hands), plus a blade.
- Attack 1: a blade cut with a clawed follow-through.
- Fire breath (their fireball): rears back and breathes a short burst of fire toward the camera.
- Hurt: a scaled flinch and a hiss.

## About the beach clip
The 6-second clip of the armoured samurai on the beach looks great as a **trailer / intro shot**. It can't be used as a fight clip: it's widescreen, has a real background, and he moves around. Fight clips need the picture as the first frame, a green background, and to end on the same pose.
