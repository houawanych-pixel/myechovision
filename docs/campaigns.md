# Story mode and drop-in campaigns

Story mode plays a **campaign**: a movie-clip intro you push through one beat at a time, forward only, that ends by
**going live** in the normal My EchoVision chat with the campaign's characters and the AI brain.

- Player: `app/story.mjs` (16:9 frame, analog stick, subtitles, characters, clip slots, import).
- Logic: `app/story-core.mjs` (manifest validator, forward-only state machine, name calling, quests). Tested in `tests/story.test.mjs`.
- Live handoff, crew and quests in chat: the end of `app/app.js` (`campaignLive`, `campaignTurn`, `campaignEvent`).
- First campaign: `app/campaigns/cryo-escape/` ("Cold Wake"). Its clip list with Grok prompts: `app/campaigns/cryo-escape/CLIPS.md`.

## How to play

Top menu → **Story** (or Create → step 4 → "▶ Story mode campaigns") → pick a campaign → **Play**.

| Control | Phone | Desktop |
|---|---|---|
| Look around (pans the frame) | Stick left / right | ← → or A / D (the stick works with the mouse too) |
| Go forward (next beat) | Push the stick up | ↑ or W |
| Back / down | Does nothing. There is no going back. | ↓ / S do nothing |
| Talk to someone | Tap them, or type "hey, Wren…" in the Talk box | Same |

While a forward or zone clip plays, pushing again does nothing; "Skip clip ▸" jumps to its end. "Clip slots" lists every
clip the campaign needs, which ones are already there, and copies the Grok prompt for each.

## Dropping in a campaign

1. Make a folder `app/campaigns/<id>/` (the id is lowercase letters, digits and dashes).
2. Put `campaign.json` in it and the clips in `app/campaigns/<id>/media/` (any path inside the folder works; the manifest names them).
3. Add one line to `app/campaigns/index.json`:
   ```json
   {"campaigns": [
     {"id": "cryo-escape", "title": "Cold Wake", "blurb": "…"},
     {"id": "my-new-one", "title": "My New One", "blurb": "One sentence for the list."}
   ]}
   ```
4. Push. It is in the Story list on the next visit. A manifest with problems opens a page listing each problem.

**Import from the device** (no push needed, stays on that device): Story → "＋ Import a campaign (.json or .zip)".
- `.json`: the manifest alone. Clips show as slots, unless `file` is a full `https://` link.
- `.zip`: the campaign folder zipped (`campaign.json` plus `media/…`). The media is stored on the device (IndexedDB),
  so it also shows in the live chat afterwards. Imported campaigns have a × to remove them.

Run `node tools/campaign-prompts.mjs app/campaigns/<id>` to (re)write `CLIPS.md` with every slot and its prompt.
Cold Wake's manifest is generated by `python3 tools/make-cold-wake.py` (edit either; the JSON is what the app reads).

## campaign.json (format `echovision-campaign/1`)

```jsonc
{
  "format": "echovision-campaign/1",
  "id": "cryo-escape",              // = folder name
  "title": "Cold Wake",
  "blurb": "One line for the list.",
  "player": {"name": "Elias Marr", "role": "Survivor", "persona": "…"},   // who you are in the live chat
  "outline": {"logline": "…", "acts": [{"title": "Act I …", "playable": true, "summary": "…"}]},
  "zones": [ … ], "beats": [ … ], "characters": [ … ], "clips": { … }, "quests": [ … ], "live": { … }
}
```

### clips: every media slot, by key

```json
"cryo_wake_loop": {"file": "media/cryo_wake_loop.mp4", "kind": "neutral", "seconds": 8, "loop": true,
                   "prompt": "What to show (used for the Grok prompt).", "standIn": "assets/inner-hall.webp"}
```
- `kind`: `neutral` (idle loop of a beat), `forward` (plays once when you push forward), `zone` (plays once when you
  enter a zone), `character` (a person on green screen).
- `file`: inside the campaign folder; `.mp4`, `.webm`, `.mov`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp` or `.gif`. A full https link also works.
- `loop` defaults to true for `neutral` and `character`. `seconds` is used in the prompt (an empty slot is shown for up to 3 s).
- `standIn` (optional): a picture or clip already in the app (path from `app/`) shown, tinted red, until the real clip
  is dropped in. Without one, the slot is a dark frame labelled `CLIP SLOT` with the file name and art description.
- `screen` (characters): `green` (default) or `blue`.

A missing file is never an error: the player shows the labelled slot and carries on.

### zones

```json
{"id": "ring-corridor", "name": "Ring Corridor", "enter": "corridor_enter_zone",
 "enterLines": [{"who": "narrator", "text": "The ring corridor. The ceiling is raining sparks."}]}
```
`enter` is the zone-enter clip; it plays when the first beat of a new zone comes up (and at the start, for the first zone).

### beats (in order; forward only)

```json
{"id": "wake", "zone": "cryo-bay", "title": "Wake",
 "neutral": "cryo_wake_loop", "forward": "cryo_climb_out_fwd",
 "cast": [{"id": "kroll", "x": 0.66, "scale": 1}],
 "lines": [{"who": "kroll", "text": "Come on, get up, let's go!", "shout": true}],
 "forwardLines": [{"who": "narrator", "text": "You drag yourself over the rim of the pod."}],
 "hint": "Push the stick up to climb out."}
```
- `neutral` loops while the player looks around; `lines` are subtitled and spoken (the phone voice, with each character's `voice.pitch`/`voice.rate`) when it starts.
- `forward` plays once on a forward push, with `forwardLines`; then the next beat's neutral loop (after its zone's enter clip if the zone changes).
- `cast`: who stands in the frame. `x` runs 0 (far left of the look-around range) to 1 (far right); `scale` sizes them for distance.
- The **last beat must have `"live": true`** and needs no forward clip; only the last beat may. Pushing forward on it goes live.
- Line `who`: a character id, `narrator` or `you`.

State machine (`story-core.mjs`): `zone` (enter clip) → `neutral` → *push* → `forward` (clip) → `zone`/`neutral` of
the next beat → … → `live`. Inputs `back`, `down` and anything unknown are ignored; `forward` is ignored while a clip plays.

### characters

```json
{"id": "wren", "name": "Wren-6", "role": "Maintenance android", "kind": "follower",
 "calls": ["Wren", "android"], "idle": "wren_idle_loop", "talk": "wren_talk", "chroma": "green",
 "persona": "You are Wren-6 …", "voice": {"pitch": 1.0, "rate": 0.95}, "fallback": "I am staying with you."}
```
- `kind: "background"`: stands where a beat's `cast` puts them, part of the scene (pans with the view).
- `kind: "follower"`: once they appear in a beat they stay with the player in every later beat (at the side of the frame, not panned) and come into the live chat if listed in `live.crew`.
- `idle`: a looping full-body clip on green (or blue) screen, keyed out on the graphics chip by the same code as the chat stage (`stage.mjs`). Missing: a dashed "CHARACTER SLOT" outline with their name.
- Call by name: `name` and every entry in `calls`. Tap a character, or type "hey, Teo" in the Talk box. They answer with the AI brain: this device's choice, otherwise the site's built-in brain from `brain.json` (Groq behind the key-keeping Worker). No key is ever in a campaign or the page. With no brain they say their `fallback` line.

### quests

```json
{"id": "dead-panel", "title": "Dead panel", "giver": "vance",
 "trigger": {"on": "live", "say": "The launch panel just died. Teo's on Bay Three. Go get him."},
 "goal":    {"find": "teo", "where": "Bay Three", "text": "Find Teo Marsh on Bay Three. Call him: \"hey, Teo\".", "say": "Panel's dead? Lead the way."},
 "return":  {"to": "vance", "words": "panel|come with me|follow me", "text": "Bring Teo back to the launch panel."},
 "outcome": {"stays": "teo", "flag": "panel-fixed", "say": "I'm staying right here until she flies.", "text": "Teo stays at the panel and works."}}
```
- `trigger.on`: `live` (when the chat starts), `zone` (with `"zone": "<id>"`), or `text` (with `"words": "regex|alternatives"`).
- `goal.find`: talking to that character (calling them by name or tapping their chip) completes the goal.
- `return`: talking to `return.to`, or saying one of `return.words` while the found character is with you, completes it.
- `outcome`: the narration is added to the chat, `stays` says their line, and from then on their AI persona knows they are at that job and stay there. `flag` is saved on the story.
- In chat the active quest shows as a strip under the crew chips.

### live (the handoff)

```json
"live": {"mode": "chat", "partner": "vance", "crew": ["vance", "wren", "teo", "bel"],
         "scene": {"name": "Hangar Two, Cairn Deep", "details": "…", "art": "assets/ship/preview.webp"},
         "line": "Welcome aboard the Long Halcyon. I'm Vance…"}
```
Going live adds the crew to the cast (ids `cmp-<campaign>-<character>`, with their idle/talk clips when those files
exist, so they appear keyed in the video view), adds the scene and the player, and starts a new story chat with
`partner` speaking `line` first. The AI brain is on when it is ready (the built-in Groq brain by default). In that chat
the crew chips switch who you talk to, typing someone's name ("hey, Teo") calls them, and quests run.

**Why chat and not the ship walk:** the chat already has everything live play needs: the AI brain, the video stage that
keys green-screen clips, expressions, voices, read-aloud and hands-free talk, and the story log. The ship walk
(`ship.html`) follows one companion and is built around Judy's clips, so a multi-character crew with quests fits the
chat best today. Walking the ship with the crew is the natural next step.

## Cold Wake (`app/campaigns/cryo-escape/`)

Original story, names and characters. It borrows only the broad shape of a "survivors against creatures and the
company" story; nothing is taken from any film or game. The ship is the app's own built-in starship.

**Playable now: Act I, the intro.** Six beats in four zones:

1. **Cryo Bay · Wake.** You wake in a cryo pod. Dace Kroll of the Kestrel Detail (mercenaries) shouts: *"Come on, get up, let's go! We don't got any time, this place is gonna blow!"* Push forward: you climb out, they run ahead.
2. **Cryo Bay · The pod row.** You lag behind. Forward: a bulkhead blast.
3. **Ring Corridor · Burning ring** (zone-enter: ceiling panels blow out). Forward: the way back collapses.
4. **Ring Corridor · The breach.** Wren-6, an android, stays with you and becomes your **follower**. You don't trust it.
5. **Maintenance Shaft C · The climb** (zone-enter: the hatch opens on fire). Forward: you climb out.
6. **Hangar Two · The survivors** (zone-enter: hangar doors part). The repair crew by their ship. Vance: *"Hey, come on, let's get on the ship now."* Push forward: **live**. Vance's first job for you is the dead launch panel (quest).

**Characters**
- **Elias Marr** (you): a quiet, stubborn man thawed out of cryo with no memory of why. The survivor who refuses to leave anyone behind.
- **Vance Okafor**, crew chief of the civilian repair tug *Long Halcyon*: fearless, steady, dry humor under fire.
- **Wren-6**, the crew's maintenance android: too still, too calm. Elias distrusts it; it earns his trust; late in the story it loses an arm and both legs saving him and Sera.
- **Teo Marsh**, engineer (the dead-panel quest). **Bel Achterberg**, rigger.
- **Dace Kroll** and **Ines Pike**, Kestrel Detail mercenaries hired by the company.
- **Sera Quill**, the love interest, found on Lantern Station after an SOS: Halcott-Vey's secret weapon, left behind.
- Enemies: **the Skein**, a species that nests in machinery; and **Halcott-Vey Consolidated**, the employer, which wants the Skein captured alive, not destroyed.

**Outline** (also in `campaign.json` → `outline.acts`)
- **Act I · Cold Wake** (playable): escape from Cairn Deep station, meet the crew, go live.
- **Act II · The Long Halcyon**: aboard the tug; distrust of Wren-6; the mercenaries came aboard too and keep asking about the station's "assets"; something from the station is in the hold.
- **Act III · The SOS**: a distress call from Lantern Station; Vance diverts; Sera Quill, sole survivor, sealed in a lab. The Skein will not go near her.
- **Act IV · Two Enemies**: Sera is the company's engineered weapon for herding the Skein. Kroll is ordered to take her and a live nest. The crew fights creatures and mercenaries at once; Wren-6 saves Elias against orders.
- **Act V · Burn the Nest**: Vance leads the push to burn the nest before the company ship arrives; Wren-6 holds a blast door and loses an arm and both legs; Elias carries it out; Sera uses what she was made for on her own terms; the crew escapes with proof.

## What is real today, and what is a placeholder

- Real: the format and validator, import (.json and .zip), the forward-only player with stick/keys/look-around, subtitles with spoken lines, labelled clip slots with stand-in art, keyed green-screen characters (as soon as their clips exist), followers that stay with you, tapping or calling characters by name through the AI brain, the live handoff into chat, crew chips, name calling and the dead-panel quest in chat.
- Placeholder: none of Cold Wake's 23 clips exist yet. Every beat shows its slot (most with the app's own corridor, hatch and ship art as a tinted stand-in). Characters show as dashed outlines until their clips are dropped in.
- Shallow on purpose: quests are text-driven in chat (calling the engineer and telling him to come is the trip to Bay Three and back); nothing moves on a map. Acts II to V are outline only.
