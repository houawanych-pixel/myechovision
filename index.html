Author Studio

Live It. Then Write It.

Every conversation becomes chapters you can review, rewrite and keep.

1
OutlineAuto-write a premise, a beginning, key events and an ending, or write your own.
2
ChaptersThe story moves chapter by chapter. Add new chapters and keep going.
3
Novel or screenplayTurn each chapter into prose or a script, in the style you choose. Rewrite any chapter.
4
ExportDownload the manuscript, the screenplay, or the original transcript.

Novel

The hatch hissed open, and cold air rolled across the floor. Kara stepped through, rifle low. “Stay close,” she said.

Yours to keep

Private by Default.

Stays on your device

Your characters, pictures, voices and stories are saved on your own phone or computer, not on our servers.

The original is never lost

Novel and screenplay versions are written alongside your transcript. Rewrites keep the earlier versions.

Respect real people

Original characters, yes. Pretending to be a real person, or cloning a voice without permission, no. Community guidelines
