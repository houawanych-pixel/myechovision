Community Guidelines

My EchoVision is for creating worlds, characters and stories. These rules keep it fun, fair and safe for everyone, including the real people whose faces and voices deserve respect.

Private by default

Every character, picture, voice and story you make starts private. Today everything is saved on your own device, and nothing is published or shared unless you choose to share it yourself (for example by exporting a file). If sharing inside My EchoVision arrives later, it will always be something you switch on for each piece of work, never the default, and these guidelines will apply to anything shared.

Real people, faces and voices

  • Original characters: invent anyone you like, realistic or animated.
  • Yourself: use your own photo or your own voice for your protagonist.
  • Ordinary resemblance: a character can look or sound like a type of person (a gravelly old captain, a bright young pilot).
  • Acting and role-play of fictional parts.
  • Parody that is clearly labelled as parody and could not be mistaken for the real person.
  • Deceptive impersonation: presenting a character as a real, identifiable person in a way meant to fool people, or putting words in a real person's mouth as if they said them.
  • Cloning a real person's voice without their permission. The voice cloner asks you to confirm you have the right to use the voice, and that confirmation must be true.
  • Using someone's photo to make them a character without their consent, or to harass, sexualise or humiliate them.
  • Sexual content of any kind involving minors, or characters presented as minors.
  • Content that targets private individuals, reveals personal information, or promotes violence or hate against people.

Stories themselves

Fiction can be dark: danger, horror, war, heartbreak and sacrifice are part of great stories. What matters is that it stays fiction and does not cross the lines above.

Reporting a problem

If you find something that breaks these guidelines, or someone is misusing your face, name or voice, tell us. Include what you saw and where. Please do not post private details about yourself or anyone else in a public report.

Report a problem

Reports are currently filed on the project's public issue tracker.

How reports are handled

  • Every report is read by a person.
  • We look at the content in context, including whether it is clearly fiction or labelled parody.
  • If something breaks the rules, it is removed or disabled, and repeated or serious misuse can lose access to shared features.
  • If you disagree with a decision, reply on the report and it will be looked at again.

These guidelines may be updated as My EchoVision grows. Last updated October 2026.
